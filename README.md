## Who We Are

We are **GTT**, a team of five combining data analysis, algorithm
development, evaluation, and engineering to explore football through
data.

We are participating in More Than a Hack 2027 at Talent Arena,
working to turn football tracking data into meaningful insights.

## What We Do

- **Data Analysis** — exploring tracking data and identifying useful
  patterns.
- **Algorithm Development** — building and evaluating methods for
  football analytics.
- **Evaluation & Validation** — testing assumptions and measuring
  performance.
- **Engineering** — making experiments reproducible and maintainable.

## Our Approach

We value reproducibility, clear documentation, collaborative
development, and evidence-based experimentation.

Our goal is not just to build a solution, but to understand why it
works, how it can fail, and how to improve it.

## The Challenge

More Than a Hack 2027 challenges teams to estimate football team
possession frame by frame using provided automatic tracking data and
pitch homography.

The competition also includes a subsequent innovation phase for
qualifying teams.
